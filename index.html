    How it works

    • Your background gives three skills at rank 1. Every level after that gives one skill point.
    • Each tree has four tiers. Advanced opens at 4 points spent in that tree, Expert at 8, Master at 12. Background skills count.
    • Rank 1 of a skill only needs its tier open. Ranks 2 to 4 also need that skill's challenge done first.

    What it assumes

    • Challenges can't be predicted, so the plan assumes you finish each one before the level you planned the rank. The list under the snapshot shows what to chase.
    • Traits are only those from character creation. Some can be removed later in the game; that isn't modelled.
    • Effects are short summaries. Exact numbers can change with game updates.
